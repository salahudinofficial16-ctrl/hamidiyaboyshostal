## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a synthesis of majestic Islamic architectural heritage, dignified academic discipline, and modern institutional trust. Designed for a male university student residence in LDA Lahore, the aesthetic establishes a brotherhood centered on moral grounding, scholarly focus, and familial warmth.

### Visual Language & Aesthetic Direction
- **Heritage Meets Modern Academia:** Balances noble traditional gravitas with structured, contemporary web standards. High legibility, crisp lines, and subtle gold ornamentation prevent the aesthetic from feeling antique or overwhelming.
- **Fraternal, Scholarly, and Disciplined:** Uses disciplined spatial grids, pristine card surfaces, and balanced contrast to convey order, security, and academic ambition.
- **Islamic Arabesque Accents:** Subtle micro-geometry (octagram stars, repeating 8-fold lattice lines, thin golden divider rules) is used sparingly as decorative headers, corner accents, watermarked backdrops, and active badge contours.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from classical Islamic manuscript illumination and collegiate institutional dignity:

- **Primary (`#064E3B` - Deep Emerald Forest Green):** Represents Islamic heritage, stability, and institutional authority. Serves as the anchor for primary navigation, top-level headings, solid action buttons, and dominant brand wrappers.
- **Secondary (`#D97706` - Warm Burnished Gold / Brass):** Evokes honor, distinction, and warmth. Applied to badges, active status indicators, key highlight trims, filigree dividers, and interactive accents.
- **Tertiary (`#047857` - Jade / Lighter Emerald):** Used for hover states on primary elements, secondary tags, positive confirmations (prayer attendance, fee clearance), and active navigational tabs.
- **Neutral (`#111827` - Deep Charcoal Ink):** Replaces harsh pure black to ensure high reading comfort for long-form notices, academic schedules, and hostel bylaws.
- **Surface Foundations:** Backgrounds default to a crisp warm off-white (`#FBFBFA`) and pure white cards (`#FFFFFF`) framed by subtle linen-toned borders (`#E5E7EB` or `#F3F4F6`), avoiding sterile digital blues in favor of warm, tactile paper tones.

## Typography

The typographic system utilizes a paired contrast between classical serif majesty and friendly contemporary precision:

- **Display & Headings (Playfair Display):** Imparts an academic, dignified aura reminiscent of classical literary institutions and Islamic scholastic tradition. Used for module headings, notice board announcements, dorm names, and high-level milestones.
- **Interface & Content (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Delivers clean readability for operational tables, room allocation listings, prayer schedules, mess menus, and student portal forms.
- **Hierarchy & Proportion:** Headings retain generous line spacing to maintain an unhurried, composed posture. Uppercase styling is reserved strictly for small brass labels, status indicators, and badges (`label-sm`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model implements a structured 12-column fluid grid system across desktop views, shifting to 6 columns on tablets and 4 columns on mobile viewports.

- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12-column layout with 24px gutters and 32px to 48px outer margins. Content containers are capped at a maximum width of 1280px to preserve comfortable line length and balanced visual composure.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 6-column layout with 20px gutters and 24px margins. Two-column card rows collapse gracefully into single or double-wide spans.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 16px gutters and 16px margins. Complex management cards stack vertically.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Maintained through multiples of 8px (`space-sm` = 8px, `space-md` = 16px, `space-lg` = 24px, `space-xl` = 40px). Section headers feature generous bottom breathing space (`space-xl`) to reinforce the architectural calm of the design system.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through soft ambient shadows paired with low-contrast, warm brass-tinted borders rather than stark greyscale drops:

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Applied to the main screen background (`#FBFBFA`) and resting container areas.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Modules):** Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`) with a delicate border (`1px solid rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.15)` or `#E5E7EB`) and an ambient shadow: `0px 2px 8px -2px rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.05), 0px 1px 3px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Dropdowns):** Subtle elevation with an expanded blur: `0px 10px 24px -4px rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.08), 0px 4px 8px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.03)`. Border shifts slightly toward warm gold: `border-color: rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.4)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Overlays):** Deep focus elevation: `0px 20px 32px -8px rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.16), 0px 8px 16px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06)`, layered above a frosted backdrop (`backdrop-filter: blur(4px); background: rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.3)`).
- **Filigree Dividers:** Section separators feature a 1px dual gradient fading from transparent to `#D97706` in the center and back to transparent, occasionally crowned with a centered 8-point gold geometric star motif.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a balanced roundedness (`roundedness: 2` = 8px default radius):

- **Standard Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Badges, Tabs):** Use `rounded-md` (8px / 0.5rem) to combine modern software usability with approachable warmth.
- **Cards & Content Containers:** Use `rounded-lg` (16px / 1rem) for an inviting, refined panel aesthetic.
- **Special Status Pills & Tags:** Use fully rounded pill shapes (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for resident status indicators (e.g., "Active Resident", "Hafiz", "Verified").
- **Islamic Ornamental Framing:** Modals and feature hero cards can feature subtle notched or clipped corners (45-degree 8px miter) or an arched top header edge to evoke traditional Islamic iwans and mihrab architectural motifs.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Background in Deep Emerald (`#064E3B`), text in pure white (`#FFFFFF`), border radius 8px, padding `10px 20px`. Hover transitions smoothly to Tertiary Green (`#047857`) with subtle gold box-shadow highlight (`0px 0px 0px 2px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.3)`).
- **Secondary / Accent Button:** Burnished Gold background (`#D97706`), text in `#FFFFFF`, hover state shifts to Deep Amber (`#B45309`).
- **Outline / Institutional Button:** Transparent background, 1.5px border in Deep Emerald (`#064E3B`), text in Deep Emerald. Hover fills with a light green wash (`rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.05)`).

### Badges & Chips
- **Gold Merit / Verification Chip:** Background in light gold tint (`#FEF3C7`), text in dark amber (`#92400E`), 1px solid border in `#F59E0B`. Used for executive body roles, merit honors, and prayer leaders.
- **Emerald Status Badge:** Background in `#ECFDF5`, text in `#064E3B`, 1px solid border in `#A7F3D0`. Used for "Room Assigned", "Fees Paid", and "In Hostel".

### Cards & Panels
- Constructed with `#FFFFFF` background, 16px corner radius, and faint gold-linen border (`1px solid #E5E7EB`).
- **Featured / Announcement Card:** Includes a top golden accent strip (3px height, gradient from `#064E3B` to `#D97706`) and an optional subtle arabesque watermarked SVG pattern on the top right corner.

### Input Fields & Selects
- Background in `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #D1D5DB`, 8px corner radius, text in `#111827`, placeholder in `#9CA3AF`.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts cleanly to Emerald (`#064E3B`) with a soft glow ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.15)`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Default unselected: 1.5px border in `#9CA3AF`, white fill.
- Selected: Emerald fill (`#064E3B`), white checkmark or center pip, accompanied by a subtle 2px amber glow ring when active.

### Lists & Notice Boards
- List rows feature clean bottom hairline borders (`#F3F4F6`), subtle gold hover indicator bar (3px width) on the left edge, and tabular lining numerals for room numbers, dates, and prayer times.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Daily Prayer & Mess Timing Strip:** A horizontal segmented card tracking Fajr through Isha and meal hours, highlighted with gold glowing pips for the current active period.
- **Resident Profile Badge:** Circular avatar encased in a 2px burnished gold ring, displaying room number and academic department in crisp small caps.
